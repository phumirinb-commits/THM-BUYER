# Pages and devices

## Phone (the main design): 390 x 844

- Screen 390 wide (`--w-screen`), content 358 inside 16 side margins (`--gutter`). Status bar 62 (`--h-status`), tab bar 80
  (`--h-tabbar`), bottom action bar 80 (`--h-actionbar`), root pages pad 112 at the bottom (`--pb-root`).
- Page shell: `<main class="screen i ...">` then `<div class="i-page">` (`.i-page--tab` on the 4 tab pages). One `h1` per
  page (visually hidden when the header shows the title).
- Tab pages (soft red top `--fx-mood`, root header `.i-hd`, tab bar): Home `f1-home`, Discover `f2-discover`,
  Order `d1-orders`, Profile `d6-profile`. Live (`f4-live`) is the middle tab and a dark page.
- Every other page: white pushed header `.i-phd` (back, title, actions), white or grey page (`--c-grey` with 8 grey bands
  between white blocks).
- Dark media pages: Live, Reels (`c2-reel`), the story viewer. No soft red top on them.

### Home, top to bottom

1. Root header on the soft red top: logo left; search button, bell, cart right.
2. Stories and lives row (`.i-stories`): your own story first, live hosts with a red Live label.
3. Badge row (`.i-promise`): ร้านทางการ · มอก. · อย. · คืนเงิน 2 เท่า.
4. Banner (`.i-banner`, 358 x 100, radius 16).
5. แบรนด์ดี (`.fx-home-brands`): store cards with the owner's photo, products, ร้านทางการ pill, ติดตาม.
6. หมวดหมู่ (`.i-cats`): starts at ทั้งหมด (a mix of every category, the default), filters in place.
7. Product grid (`.i-grid2`, masonry product cards).

### Search

The Home search button opens the search page (`a1-search`): the field takes focus as the page opens so the keyboard
comes up. Recents, trending, categories. Photo search starts from the camera on the search page. No running light on any
search field; the tap focus ring stays.

## Desktop: 1440 wide (also clean at 1280, never a sideways scroll)

`proto/final-desk/` (generated by `link-tools/fin/desk/gen.mjs`, shared parts in `fd.js`, layout in `desk.css`): the same
kit classes and tokens grown for 1440. Pages: หน้าแรก `home.html`, หน้าสินค้า `product.html`, หน้าร้าน `shop.html`, ไลฟ์ `live.html`, Discover `discover.html`, คำสั่งซื้อ `orders.html`, โปรไฟล์ `profile.html`. One-row top bar with a search button next to the bell; it
opens a search layer from the top with the cursor in the field (Esc closes): recents, trending, categories, live product
results. The soft red top only on the tab pages (`body.fd--home`, `fd--disc`, `fd--orders`, `fd--profile`).

## iPhone Duo: 3 poses

`proto/final-duo/` (generated by `link-tools/fin/duo/gen.mjs`), the same 7 pages as Desktop. Poses: พับ 466 x 678 (one pane, the rail on the right) · กางแนวนอน 951 x 669 (the main page in the right pane, its companion in the left, the fold between) · กางแนวตั้ง 669 x 951 (one wider pane, bars top and bottom).
The search button sits in the rail (พับ, กางแนวนอน) or the toolbar (กางแนวตั้ง); in กางแนวนอน the search panel takes the
left pane and Home stays on the right. Soft red top only on `.fu-home`, `.fu-disc`, `.fu-ord`, `.fu-prof`.
